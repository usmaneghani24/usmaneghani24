## 🟢 Haazir — HR Management, by eSkills Tech

Attendance and leave, without the paperwork. Staff check in from their phone, HR approves leave and fixes records, and every change is logged.

- 📱 **Check in and out** from any phone, on server time, with lateness worked out automatically
- 🗓️ **Leave requests** with working days counted and one-tap approval for HR
- 🧾 **Audit log** of every correction, approval and holiday change
- 🔒 **Built to be trusted:** session login, rate limits, strict security headers, encrypted nightly backups, and 1,700+ automated tests
